## Quick Recap — Opinion Essay

> Introduction → state the issue + your opinion clearly
> Body → First of all, / In addition, + Point → Evidence/Example → Explain
> Conclusion → In conclusion, + restate your opinion

> 🎨 **Thiết kế:** 1 thẻ duy nhất, gọn, dạng flashcard tra cứu nhanh (sticky) — KHÔNG dùng bảng Formula/Example/Not good đầy đủ như Lớp 11.

## Bảng tín hiệu nhận diện — bản mở rộng (Mastery)

| Function | Signal (nhiều lựa chọn, tránh lặp) |
|---|---|
| State opinion | I believe.../I think.../In my opinion.../From my point of view... |
| First reason | First of all, / Firstly, / To begin with, |
| Second reason | In addition, / Furthermore, / Moreover, / Secondly, |
| Third reason (nếu có) | Finally, / Last but not least, |
| Give an example | For example, / For instance, / A clear example of this is... |
| Show contrast/counter-point | However, / On the other hand, / That said, |
| Conclude | In conclusion, / To conclude, / Overall, / Taking everything into account, |
| ⚠️ Tránh (văn nói) | don't/can't → do not/cannot |

*💡 Lưu ý Mastery: KHÔNG dùng lại đúng 1 cụm 2 lần trong cùng 1 bài luận (VD nếu Reason 1 dùng "First of all,", Reason 2 nên đổi sang "Furthermore," thay vì lặp công thức cũ).*

## [Structure · Passage Arrangement — Bài 1] — Culture shock (dễ)

Put the sentences in the correct order to form a complete paragraph.

A. Should young people who move to a new country adopt the local culture completely, or keep their own traditions? In my opinion, they should try to do both.
B. To begin with, learning the local culture helps newcomers integrate more easily into their new community.
C. For example, understanding local customs can make it easier to make friends and find opportunities at work or school.
D. At the same time, however, keeping some of their own traditions helps them stay connected to their identity and family background.
E. In conclusion, a healthy balance between adapting and preserving one's roots seems to be the most realistic solution.

**Answer: A → B → C → D → E**

Giải thích: A nêu vấn đề + quan điểm → mở đầu. B dùng "To begin with," → lý do 1. C dùng "For example," + lặp "local culture/customs" từ B → sau B. D dùng "At the same time, however," chuyển sang lý do 2 → sau C. E có "In conclusion," → cuối cùng.

## [Structure · Passage Arrangement — Bài 2] — Social media (khó hơn)

Put the sentences in the correct order to form a complete paragraph.

A. This question is becoming increasingly relevant as billions of people connect through platforms that were unimaginable a generation ago.
B. Does social media make the world more connected, or does it make cultural differences disappear?
C. On the other hand, algorithms often push the same trending content to everyone, which can make cultures start to look increasingly similar.
D. There is no simple answer, but the truth probably lies somewhere between these two extremes.

**Answer: B → A → E → C → D**

## Guided Writing — Da Nang International Fireworks Festival

> You just saw one example of local culture being shared with the world. World festivals work in a similar way, but in the opposite direction — foreign traditions travelling into Vietnamese life. Let's look at that side of the story.

> 🎨 **Thiết kế:** banner nhỏ đầu mục minh hoạ pháo hoa đêm (xem prompt ảnh ở đầu file, mục Ảnh cần sinh) — vẫn dùng đúng 2 màu accent `--slate`/`--terracotta` đã thiết lập ở Hook để giữ cảm giác liền mạch thị giác giữa Part 1 và Part 3, dù nội dung/chủ đề đã đổi hẳn sang world festivals.

*Question: Da Nang hosts an International Fireworks Festival every year, where teams from different countries around the world come to compete. Should Vietnamese cities continue to host more international festivals like this?*

| Reason | Example | Explain |
|---|---|---|
| Hosting international festivals like this gives local people the chance to experience culture from around the world without leaving home | Teams from countries such as Italy, France, and the UK bring their own music and fireworks styles to Da Nang every year | Broadens local people's understanding of different cultures and artistic traditions |
| This kind of festival brings a real boost to the local economy and gives residents something to be proud of | Hotels, restaurants, and local businesses in Da Nang see a large increase in visitors during the festival every year | Creates jobs and helps the city become known internationally |

> Da Nang hosts an International Fireworks Festival every year, where teams from different countries around the world come to compete. In my opinion, ________________________________________________ To begin with, ________________________________________________ Furthermore, ________________________________________________ In conclusion, ________________________________________________

*Target: 180-200 words.*

**Model answer (shown after submission):**

> Da Nang hosts an International Fireworks Festival every year, where teams from different countries around the world come to compete. In my opinion, Vietnamese cities should continue to host more international festivals like this.
>
> To begin with, hosting international festivals like this gives local people the chance to experience culture from around the world without leaving home. For example, teams from countries such as Italy, France, and the UK bring their own music and fireworks styles to Da Nang every year. This broadens local people's understanding of different cultures and artistic traditions.
>
> Furthermore, this kind of festival brings a real boost to the local economy and gives residents something to be proud of. For instance, hotels, restaurants, and local businesses in Da Nang see a large increase in visitors during the festival every year, which creates jobs and helps the city become known internationally as a modern, exciting destination.
>
> In conclusion, although hosting large international events can sometimes overshadow smaller local festivals, I believe the cultural exchange and economic benefits make events like the Da Nang Fireworks Festival well worth continuing.
>
> *(181 words)*

**Self-check (không phải Athena chấm):**
- Có nêu rõ quan điểm ngay ở Introduction không?
- Có dùng connector đa dạng (không lặp First of all/In addition) không?
- Conclusion có thừa nhận ý kiến trái chiều trước khi chốt quan điểm không?
