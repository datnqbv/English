Activity 1 • Structure Labelling (Village Life on Social Media)
Match each paragraph (1-4) with the correct description (A-D):
Đoạn 1
"Nowadays, more and more remote communities in Vietnam are sharing their everyday life on social media, and one family living in a mountain village recently became known around the world this way. In my opinion, this trend brings more benefits than drawbacks to young Vietnamese people."
Đoạn 2
"To begin with, sharing traditional village life online helps connect remote communities with the rest of the world. For instance, viewers from other countries can learn about customs and traditions they would otherwise never encounter. This exposure can bring real economic benefits, as increased attention often leads to tourism and additional income for local families."
Đoạn 3
"Furthermore, this kind of content can actually help preserve culture rather than threaten it. When young people record their daily routines, they are documenting traditional skills and customs that might otherwise be forgotten. As a result, future generations will still have access to this knowledge, even as lifestyles continue to change."
Đoạn 4
"In conclusion, although some people worry that filming daily life for an online audience could encourage communities to change their habits, I believe the benefits of connecting with a wider audience and preserving culture for future generations far outweigh this risk."
