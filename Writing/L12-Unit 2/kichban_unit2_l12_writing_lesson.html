Language Signals for MasteryNhiều lựa chọn để tránh lặp connector

State an opinionI believe... · I think... · In my opinion... · From my point of view...
First reasonFirst of all, · Firstly, · To begin with,
Second reasonIn addition, · Furthermore, · Moreover, · Secondly,
Third reasonFinally, · Last but not least,
Give an exampleFor example, · For instance, · A clear example of this is...
Contrast / counter-pointHowever, · On the other hand, · That said,
ConcludeIn conclusion, · To conclude, · Overall, · Taking everything into account,
Avoid spoken contractionsdon’t → do not · can’t → cannot

Do not repeat the same connector twice in one essay. If Reason 1 uses “To begin with,” choose “Furthermore,” or another suitable option for Reason 2.

Mastery Check

A strong opinion essay makes its position clear, develops reasons with evidence, varies connectors, and closes with a thoughtful counter-point.
