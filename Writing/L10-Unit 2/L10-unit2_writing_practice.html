Several things we can do to make our school greener and cleaner.

• First: plant small trees in classrooms (improve air quality).

📝 Model Answer (Bài mẫu tham khảo)

There are several things we can do to make our school greener and cleaner. First, we should plant small trees and potted flowers in our classrooms. For example, students can take turns watering them every morning. This can improve indoor air quality and create a fresh learning space. Second, the school could organise a paper-recycling programme. For example, classrooms can collect used notebooks and cardboard boxes instead of throwing them into the trash bin. This will save a huge amount of paper and protect forests. Third, we should encourage students to walk or cycle to school whenever possible. This will reduce carbon emissions around our school gates. In conclusion, protecting our school environment starts with simple daily actions.
