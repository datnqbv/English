Part-Time Job While in School

[1] Should teenagers be allowed to get a part-time job while still in school? I believe students should be allowed to work part-time, for the following reasons.
[2] First of all, a part-time job can teach teenagers important life skills. For example, working in a shop or a cafe helps them learn how to manage time and communicate with customers. This can prepare them well for the future.
[3] In addition, earning their own money can make teenagers more responsible. For instance, students who earn pocket money often learn to plan their spending more carefully. This can help them understand the value of money.
[4] In conclusion, although schoolwork should still come first, I think a part-time job can bring real benefits to teenagers.
State opinion: I believe... / I think... / In my opinion...
First reason: First of all, / One advantage is...
Second reason: In addition, / Also, / Furthermore,
Give example: For example, / For instance,
Conclude: In conclusion, / Overall,

Model Answer

Model Answer — Social Media Accounts

Should teenagers be allowed to have their own social media accounts? I believe teenagers should be allowed to use social media, for the following reasons. First of all, social media helps teenagers stay connected with friends and family. For example, they can share updates and photos easily, even when they live far away or have a busy schedule. This can help them feel less isolated, especially if their family does not live nearby. In addition, social media can also help teenagers learn new things. For instance, many teenagers follow educational pages or watch tutorial videos online in their free time. This can support their learning outside school. In conclusion, although parents should still guide how it is used, I think social media can bring real benefits to teenagers.

(128 words)

Model Answer — Choosing Own Clothes

Should teenagers be allowed to choose their own clothes without their parents' approval? I believe teenagers should be allowed to choose their own clothes, for the following reasons. First of all, choosing their own clothes helps teenagers express their personal identity. For example, a student who prefers simple, comfortable outfits should not be forced to wear something more formal just because their parents prefer it. This can help them feel more confident about who they are. In addition, making small decisions like this can help teenagers develop responsibility. For instance, learning to choose clothes suitable for different occasions, such as school or a family event, is a useful life skill. In conclusion, although parents can still offer advice, I think teenagers should have the final say in what they wear.

(135 words)

Model Answer — Limiting Teenagers' Screen Time

Should parents limit teenagers' screen time? I believe parents should set some limits on how much time teenagers spend on screens, for the following reasons. First of all, too much screen time can affect teenagers' health. For example, spending hours looking at a screen every day can cause eye strain and make it harder to sleep at night. This can affect their ability to concentrate at school the next day. In addition, limiting screen time can help teenagers spend more time on other activities. For instance, they could spend more time exercising, reading, or talking with their family instead. This can help them develop a more balanced lifestyle. In conclusion, although screens can be useful, I think parents should still help teenagers manage their screen time for the sake of their health and daily life.

(135 words)
