Generation Gap • Opinion Essay
📝 Opinion Essay 🎯 Target: 120–150 words 📊 4 Phases: Identify • Structure • Write • Production

Identify: Đọc bài luận mẫu về việc học sinh làm thêm để ghép nối 4 đoạn văn lớn với mô tả chức năng tương ứng, sau đó phân loại các lý do ủng hộ / phản đối giờ giới nghiêm.

Activity 1 • Structure Labelling (Cấp đoạn văn)
Read the opinion essay below. Match each paragraph (1-4) with the correct description (A-D):
Đoạn 1
"Should teenagers be allowed to get a part-time job while still in school? I believe students should be allowed to work part-time, for the following reasons."
Đoạn 2
"First of all, a part-time job can teach teenagers important life skills. For example, working in a shop or a cafe helps them learn how to manage time and communicate with customers. This can prepare them well for the future."
Đoạn 3
"In addition, earning their own money can make teenagers more responsible. For instance, students who earn pocket money often learn to plan their spending more carefully. This can help them understand the value of money."
Đoạn 4
"In conclusion, although schoolwork should still come first, I think a part-time job can bring real benefits to teenagers."

📌 Topic: Should teenagers be allowed to have a curfew set by their parents?

Supports a curfew (Ủng hộ giờ giới nghiêm)
Against a curfew (Phản đối giờ giới nghiêm)

Structure: Chạm vào các câu bên dưới theo đúng thứ tự logic (Introduction → Reason 1 → Reason 2 → Conclusion) để tạo thành bài luận hoàn chỉnh.
