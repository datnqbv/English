*🎨 Thiết kế: bảng 3 hàng, mỗi hàng có icon riêng (📍Opening/🎵Body/💬Closing); chạm 1 hàng thì đoạn tương ứng trong bài mẫu bên dưới sáng viền, liên kết trực quan bảng ↔ bài mẫu.*

### Model vs. Not Good — Opening

| ✅ Good | ❌ Not good |
|---|---|
| "Last Saturday, I went to the Summer Music Night at Riverside Park with my brother." | "I want to talk about a music event." |
| Có đủ Event + When + Where + Who with, câu cụ thể ngay từ đầu. | Chung chung, thiếu cả 4 yếu tố — người đọc không hình dung được gì. |

### Model vs. Not Good — Closing

| ✅ Good | ❌ Not good |
|---|---|
| "By the end of the night, I felt amazing, and I can't wait to go again next summer." | "It was fun. The end." |
| Có tính từ cảm xúc cụ thể (amazing) + to-infinitive thể hiện mong muốn (can't wait to go). | Cộc lốc, không tính từ, không cảm xúc thật. |

### Brainstorm cho phần Body (khó nhất)

**Formula: [Atmosphere] + [Activity 1] + [Activity 2] + [A special/favourite moment]**

1. Chọn 1 tính từ tả không khí (atmosphere) đúng với sự kiện.
2. Liệt kê 2 hoạt động cụ thể đã làm (What we did).
3. Chọn 1 khoảnh khắc đặc biệt/thích nhất.
4. Ráp thành câu, dùng compound sentence (and/but/so) để nối ý.
5. Đọc lại — có bị liệt kê khô khan không? Nếu có, thêm 1 chi tiết cảm giác/hành động nhỏ.

*⚠️ Lỗi thường gặp: liệt kê hoạt động như 1 danh sách ("We sang. We danced. We ate.") thay vì nối bằng compound sentence tự nhiên.*

### Phân tích bài mẫu hoàn chỉnh

**①** Last Saturday, I went to the Summer Music Night at Riverside Park with my brother. It was a small outdoor concert, **②** and I was really excited because it was my first time seeing a live band play at night. **③** The atmosphere was warm and relaxed, so everyone just sat on the grass and enjoyed the cool evening air. We listened to three local bands, and I even got the chance to try singing on stage during an open mic! My brother helped me carry my guitar onto the stage, and he wanted to record every song, but his phone battery died halfway through. **④** By the end of the night, I felt amazing, and I can't wait to go again next summer.

*(121 words)*

| # | Nhãn | Ghi chú |
|---|---|---|
| ① | Opening | Đủ Event/When/Where/Who with trong 1 câu |
| ② | Body | compound sentence với "and" |
| ③ | Body | target vocab (atmosphere, relaxed) + compound sentence "so" |
| ④ | Closing | target vocab (amazing) + to-infinitive "can't wait to go" |

Điểm ngữ pháp lồng ghép: compound sentences (and/so/but), to-infinitive ("the chance to try singing", "can't wait to go"), bare infinitive sau "help" ("helped me carry").

### Useful Vocabulary

| Location | Atmosphere | Activities | Feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| park, riverside, stadium, hall | relaxed, lively, exciting, warm | play/sing on stage, listen to a band, try an open mic | excited, amazing, proud |

*In đậm = 4 từ mục tiêu thật của Unit theo syllabus: atmosphere, relaxed, excited, amazing — các từ còn lại là bổ trợ, không vượt trần A2.*

### Bảng tín hiệu nhận diện

| Function | Signal |
|---|---|
| Opening | Last + time phrase (Last Saturday/month), at/in + place |
| Connect ideas (Body) | and / but / so |
| Express a plan/wish (Closing) | can't wait to + V |
| Bare infinitive after help | help + somebody + V (no "to") |

**[Structure · Passage Arrangement — Bài 1] — School talent show (dễ)**

Put the sentences in the correct order to form a complete blog post.

A. Then, we watched some amazing performances, including singing, dancing, and even a magic act.
B. By the end of the night, I felt proud and happy that we shared such a fun evening.
C. Last Friday, my classmates and I went to the school talent show in the school hall.
D. After the show, we all took photos together on the stage.
E. First, the atmosphere was exciting and a little noisy, with everyone cheering for their friends.

**Answer: C → E → A → D → B**

Giải thích: C nêu đủ 4 yếu tố mở đầu → đứng đầu. E dùng "First," → ngay sau C. A dùng "Then," → tiếp nối E. D dùng "After the show," quy chiếu sự kiện đã kết thúc → sau A. B có "By the end of the night," → luôn cuối cùng.

**[Structure · Passage Arrangement — Bài 2] — Family movie night (khó hơn)**

Put the sentences in the correct order to form a complete blog post.

A. Her tears surprised me at first, yet I found myself smiling because the story was truly **touching** *(cảm động)*.
B. My little sister **kept asking** to watch a new family movie, so we finally went to the cinema together last weekend.
C. Looking back, I'm really glad we chose to watch that film together, and I hope to make more memories like this with her.
D. The cinema was warm and cosy, and the smell of popcorn filled the air as soon as we walked in.
E. Halfway through the film, she started crying quietly, but she still refused to leave her seat.

**Answer: B → D → E → A → C**

Giải thích: B dùng "so" thiết lập bối cảnh → mở đầu. D mô tả không khí ngay khi bước vào → sau B. E giới thiệu chi tiết "she started crying" → sau D. A dùng đại từ quy chiếu "Her tears" (nhắc lại E) + "yet" → phải đứng sau E. C có "Looking back," → kết luận.

*(Bài 2 khó hơn: không dùng First/Then tường minh, dựa vào đại từ quy chiếu "Her tears" và liên từ "yet" — đồng thời lồng đúng compound sentences (so/and/but/yet) và to-infinitive (asking to watch, refused to leave, chose to watch, hope to make) là trọng tâm ngữ pháp Unit 3.)*

*(Đã sửa: câu B bản gốc dùng "had been asking...for weeks" — quá khứ hoàn thành tiếp diễn, chưa từng dạy ở Unit này — đổi thành "kept asking" để không vượt phạm vi ngữ pháp đã công bố, vẫn giữ đúng cụm to-infinitive "asking to watch".)*

### Writing Rubric (Athena chấm Free Writing)

| Criteria | Scale | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Task Achievement | 1-4 | Includes event, when/where/who with, atmosphere, activities, and feelings |
| 2. Organisation | 1-4 | Clear Opening, Body, and Closing; ideas flow logically |
| 3. Language Use | 1-4 | Accurate past tense, correct use of compound sentences (and/but/so) and to-infinitive/bare infinitive |
| 4. Length & Fluency | 1-4 | Reaches about 120 words, sentences are natural and well-connected |

### Self-evaluation

- ☐ I can identify the different parts of a blog post (Opening/Body/Closing).
- ☐ I can use compound sentences (and/but/so) and to-infinitive/bare infinitive correctly.
- ☐ I can arrange sentences into a well-structured blog post.
- ☐ I can write a complete blog post about an event I attended or a show I watched, describing the atmosphere, activities, and my feelings.
- ☐ My writing reaches the required word count (about 100-130 words) and reads naturally.
