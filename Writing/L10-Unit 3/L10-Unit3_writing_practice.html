Chế độ xem trên di động:

Water Puppet Show (Múa rối nước)

“Two weekends ago, I went to a water puppet show at Thang Long Theatre with my mum. The theatre was crowded and lively, so we had to sit near the back. The puppets moved on the water while musicians played live behind a bamboo screen. I loved how colourful and funny some of the characters were. By the end of the show, I felt amazed, and I can't wait to bring my little cousin next time.”

🎯 Bố cục bài đọc:

  • Opening: Two weekends ago, Thang Long Theatre, with mum.
  • Body: Crowded and lively; puppets on water with live music.
  • Closing: Amazed, can't wait to bring cousin next time.

Summer Music Night (Bài mẫu 121 từ)

“Last Saturday, I went to the Summer Music Night at Riverside Park with my brother. It was a small outdoor concert, and I was really excited because it was my first time seeing a live band play at night. The atmosphere was warm and relaxed, so everyone just sat on the grass and enjoyed the cool evening air. We listened to three local bands, and I even got the chance to try singing on stage during an open mic! My brother helped me carry my guitar onto the stage, and he wanted to record every song, but his phone battery died halfway through. By the end of the night, I felt amazing, and I can't wait to go again next summer.”

Useful Vocabulary & Signals

✨ 4 Từ mục tiêu:

atmosphere (không khí), relaxed (thư giãn), excited (hào hứng), amazing (tuyệt vời).

🔗 Từ tín hiệu & Cấu trúc:

• Last Saturday / Last month (Opening)
• and / but / so (Body)
• can't wait to + V (Closing)
• help + sb + V (nguyên mẫu)

📝 Model Answer (Bài mẫu tham khảo)

Last Friday, I went to a horror movie night at the cinema with two of my classmates. We arrived a little early and bought some popcorn before the film started. The cinema was dark and a bit scary, so we sat close together the whole time. We watched a scary film about a ghost in an old house, and we screamed so loudly that other people started laughing at us. My friend wanted to close her eyes during the scariest part, but she couldn't stop watching. By the end of the film, I felt very scared but excited, and I really want to go again, but maybe with more friends next time.
