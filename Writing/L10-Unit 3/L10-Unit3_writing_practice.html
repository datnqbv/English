Model answer

Last Friday, I went to a horror movie night at the cinema with two of my classmates. We arrived a little early and bought some popcorn before the film started. The cinema was dark and a bit scary, so we sat close together the whole time. We watched a scary film about a ghost in an old house, and we screamed so loudly that other people started laughing at us. My friend wanted to close her eyes during the scariest part, but she couldn't stop watching. By the end of the film, I felt very scared but excited, and I really want to go again, but maybe with more friends next time.

We watched several bands play cover songs, and I even joined in singing during the last song of the night. My friend recorded a short video on her phone so we could watch it again later. By the end of the festival, I felt proud and happy, and I can't wait to join the festival again next year.

Last summer holiday, my family and I went to a water fountain show by the river in our city. It usually starts at 8 p.m., and the area was already crowded with visitors by the time we arrived. The atmosphere was magical and lively, with colourful lights dancing along with the music. We watched the water jets rise and fall in time with each song, and my little brother clapped every time the lights changed colour. I even took a short video to show my friends later. By the end of the show, I felt amazed, and I really hope to watch it again next summer.
