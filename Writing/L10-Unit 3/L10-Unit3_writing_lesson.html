A Music Event Experience

Learn how to turn a memorable music event into a vivid, well-structured blog post.

Write a blog post (about 120 words) about an experience at a music event.
Use descriptive adjectives and appropriate blog structure.
Warm-up

The first time I did it

LanIntroduction
The first time I stood in a stadium with 20,000 people, lightsticks glowing everywhere, and 30 idols walked out on stage — I nearly cried. Last month, I went to the Anh Trai Say Hi concert in Ha Noi with my best friend. We had waited outside the gate since 4 p.m., and when the show finally started, the whole crowd sang every word together. I'd never felt anything like it before.

— Lan

Everyone has their own “first time” music memory — it doesn't have to be a huge concert like Lan's. What's YOUR first-time music memory?

Structure

Study the model blog post

Tap a part to highlight its role in the model.

Last Saturday, I went to the Summer Music Night at Riverside Park with my brother. The atmosphere was warm and relaxed, so everyone sat on the grass. We listened to local bands, sang and took photos together. By the end of the night, I felt amazing, and I can't wait to go again next summer.
Compare

Model vs. Not Good

Opening

✅ Good

“Last Saturday, I went to the Summer Music Night at Riverside Park with my brother.”

Có đủ Event + When + Where + Who with, câu cụ thể ngay từ đầu.

❌ Not good

“I want to talk about a music event.”

Chung chung, thiếu cả 4 yếu tố — người đọc không hình dung được gì.

Closing

✅ Good

“By the end of the night, I felt amazing, and I can't wait to go again next summer.”

Có tính từ cảm xúc cụ thể (amazing) + to-infinitive thể hiện mong muốn (can't wait to go).

❌ Not good

“It was fun. The end.”

Cộc lốc, không tính từ, không cảm xúc thật.

Brainstorm

Brainstorm cho phần Body

AtmosphereActivity 1Activity 2A special/favourite moment
Chọn 1 tính từ tả không khí (atmosphere) đúng với sự kiện.
Liệt kê 2 hoạt động cụ thể đã làm (What we did).
Chọn 1 khoảnh khắc đặc biệt/thích nhất.
Ráp thành câu, dùng compound sentence (and/but/so) để nối ý.
Đọc lại — có bị liệt kê khô khan không? Nếu có, thêm 1 chi tiết cảm giác/hành động nhỏ.

⚠️ Lỗi thường gặp: liệt kê hoạt động như 1 danh sách (“We sang. We danced. We ate.”) thay vì nối bằng compound sentence tự nhiên.

Worked example

Phân tích bài mẫu hoàn chỉnh

Tap ①–④ to focus on one writing feature.

Last Saturday, I went to the Summer Music Night at Riverside Park with my brother. It was a small outdoor concert, and I was really excited because it was my first time seeing a live band play at night. The atmosphere was warm and relaxed, so everyone just sat on the grass and enjoyed the cool evening air. We listened to three local bands, and I even got the chance to try singing on stage during an open mic! My brother helped me carry my guitar onto the stage, and he wanted to record every song, but his phone battery died halfway through. By the end of the night, I felt amazing, and I can't wait to go again next summer.
