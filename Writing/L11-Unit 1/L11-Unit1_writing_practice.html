Model Answer

Đáp án gợi ý bảng trích xuất:

  • (1) Accept: That sounds like a great idea, I'm in!
  • (2) Ask info 1: How many steps do we need each day?
  • (3) Ask info 2: Is there a group chat to track our progress?
  • (4) Closing: Talk to you soon!

💡 Lưu ý: Bài mẫu hỏi 2 thông tin trong 1 câu ghép bằng "and" — khi trích xuất tách thành 2 câu hỏi riêng.

Đáp án gợi ý câu chức năng:

  1. 1. I'd love to come to your birthday party.
  2. 2. What time should I arrive?
  3. 3. That sounds great, I'd love to join!
  4. 4. Do I need to bring anything?
  5. 5. Where should I meet you?
