Activity 7 • Guided Writing (Full Support • 3 Situations)
Bài 1 · Lan invited you to a yoga class:
"Hey Minh, I'm going to a yoga class this Sunday morning. Do you want to come with me?"
Gợi ý: Accept the invitation • Ask what time the class starts • Ask if you need to bring your own yoga mat.
Bài 2 · Nam invited you to a healthy cooking class:
"Hey Minh, I'm trying out a new healthy cooking class this Friday evening. Want to join me?"
Gợi ý: Accept the invitation • Ask what time the class starts • Ask if you need to bring any ingredients.
Bài 3 · Lan invited you to a morning jogging group:
"Hey Minh, I'm joining a morning jogging group around the park every Sunday. Want to come along?"
Gợi ý: Accept the invitation • Ask what time the group meets • Ask if you need any special shoes.

Activity 11 • Guided Writing (Advanced Situations)
Bài 1 · Nam's motivation meetup:
"Hey Minh, I know it's been hard for you lately — two months of working out with no results, plus a busy school schedule! I'm going to a weekend meetup where a trainer talks about how to stay motivated. Want to come with me?"
Include: Accept • Ask what time it starts • Ask if you need to sign up before going.
Bài 2 · Lan's free health check-up camp:
"Hey Minh, I'm going to a free health check-up camp at the community centre this Saturday. A doctor checks your height, weight, and blood pressure for free. Want to come with me?"
Include: Accept • Ask what time it starts • Ask if you need to bring your health insurance card.

Activity 12 • Free Writing (Graded by AI Athena • 3 Tasks)
FW1 • Healthy people sharing session
"Hi! Our class is having a sharing session about people around us who live a healthy life. Do you want to come and share your story too?"
💡 Idea prompts:
  • Think about what you'd want to know before joining (when it happens, where, or how to prepare).
  • Choose 2 pieces of information that matter most to you.
  • Keep your message friendly and informal, just like chatting with a classmate.
0 từ / mục tiêu 35–45 từ
FW2 • New healthy juice bar
"Hey! There's a new juice bar that opened near school with really healthy smoothies. Want to check it out with me after school?"
💡 Idea prompts:
  • Think about what you'd want to know about the juice bar (opening hours, price, what's on the menu...).
  • Choose 2 pieces of information that matter most to you.
  • Keep your reply short and casual — this is a message to a friend, not a formal reply.
0 từ / mục tiêu 35–45 từ
FW3 • Weekend cycling trip
"A few of us are going cycling around the lake this weekend to stay fit. Would you like to join us?"
💡 Idea prompts:
  • Think about what you'd want to know before joining (meeting time, meeting point, what to bring...).
  • Choose 2 pieces of information that matter most to you.
  • Remember your message still needs a Greeting, Accept, 2 questions, Closing, and Sign-off.
0 từ / mục tiêu 35–45 từ
