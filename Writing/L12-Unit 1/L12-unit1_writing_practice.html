Đoạn 1
"Alexandre Yersin is remembered around the world as one of the greatest medical researchers of the nineteenth century. He was born in Switzerland in 1863. His mother, a schoolteacher, raised him and encouraged him to study science. While he was studying medicine in Paris, he worked closely with famous scientists such as Louis Pasteur and Émile Roux."
Đoạn 2
"Yersin's most famous achievement came in 1894, when he travelled to Hong Kong during a deadly plague outbreak. Working in a small, poorly equipped hut, he identified the bacterium responsible for the disease, which was later named Yersinia pestis in his honour. This discovery eventually helped doctors develop treatments that saved countless lives."
Đoạn 3
"In 1895, Yersin settled in Nha Trang, Vietnam, where he founded a branch of the Pasteur Institute. While living there, he also explored the surrounding highlands and recommended the site that later became the town of Da Lat. He never married, choosing instead to devote his life to scientific research and to the local community."
Đoạn 4
"Yersin died in Nha Trang in 1943, at the age of 79. However, his legacy lives on: streets, schools, and hospitals across Vietnam still carry his name, and local people continue to visit his grave every year."
