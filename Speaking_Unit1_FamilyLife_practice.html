Family Routines — Luyện tập & Nói

Why should/shouldn't children do housework?

Stating Opinion: I think (that)... · In my opinion,... · I believe (that)...
Agreeing: I agree (with you). · That's right.
Disagreeing: I don't think so. · I don't agree.
Should:
  • ...it makes them feel proud of contributing something real to the family.
  • ...it gives them practical experience that textbooks can't teach.
  • ...it helps them understand how much effort it takes to run a home.
Shouldn't:
  • ...their schedules are already packed with classes and extracurricular activities.
  • ...some tasks aren't safe for young children.
  • ...it can take time away from hobbies that help them grow in other ways.

Luyện tập

Chọn đáp án đúng cho mỗi câu.

Xác định chức năng giao tiếp
1. "In my opinion, children should help with housework."
2. "I think you're right — housework really does teach responsibility."
3. "I don't think children should do a lot of housework, but a little can be helpful."
💡 Ghi nhớ: một câu có thể vừa mang chức năng đồng ý/phản đối, vừa chứa thêm một ý kiến riêng đi kèm — hãy đọc kỹ cả câu trước khi chọn.
Chọn phản hồi phù hợp
4. Lan: Do you think children should help with housework?
5. Minh: I think kids should do more chores at home.
— chọn câu đồng ý với Minh —
6. Linh: I don't think children should do housework — they need more time to study.
— chọn câu vừa phản đối Linh, vừa nêu lý do riêng —
💡 Ghi nhớ: khi được yêu cầu vừa đồng ý/phản đối vừa nêu lý do, câu trả lời đúng phải có đủ cả 2 phần — chỉ có 1 phần thôi là chưa đủ.

Production

This is a self-study platform — every task below is speaking practice with Athena: record your answer and receive automatic feedback.

Athena asks — 2 lượt hỏi đáp

Athena sẽ hỏi 2 câu. Mỗi lượt, bạn chọn đáp án phù hợp rồi ghi âm nói to câu đó — không cần gõ chữ, chỉ cần chọn và nói.

Athena — Lượt 1

"Do you think children should do housework?"

Chọn "I agree" hoặc "I disagree" để xem câu bạn sẽ nói.
Athena — Lượt 2

"Why do you think that?"

Chọn 1 lý do ở trên để xem câu bạn sẽ nói.
Lượt 1I think children should do housework.
Lượt 2In my opinion, it gives them practical experience they can't get from a textbook.
Athena responds

Đây không phải câu mẫu để đọc lại — mỗi lượt chỉ gợi ý một vài phrase/collocation và từ nối, bạn tự dựng câu của riêng mình từ đó. 3 lượt nói đi nói lại với Athena.

Athena — as Linh (lượt 1)

"I don't think children should do housework — our schedules are already packed with school and activities."

Trả lời lại Linh: nêu ý kiến của bạn (should/shouldn't) kèm 2 lý do.
🔑 Phrase gợi ý: proud to contribute practical experience run a home packed with activities not safe for children
💬 Từ nối: First, Also,
Athena — as Linh (lượt 2)

"Hmm, maybe. But I still think studying should come first."

Agree/Disagree với Linh, kèm 1 lý do bổ sung.
🔑 Phrase gợi ý: matter a lot relax and unwind manage time better teach responsibility
💬 Từ nối: However, That's why
Athena — as Linh (lượt 3)

"That's a fair point, actually. But what would you say to a friend who thinks housework is a waste of time?"

Trả lời câu hỏi của Linh, rồi chốt lại ý kiến của bạn.
🔑 Phrase gợi ý: doesn't take much time worth doing a little waste of time parents' job
💬 Từ nối: So, In conclusion,
Turn 1: "I don't agree. First, doing housework makes kids feel proud to contribute something real to the family. Also, it teaches them skills that textbooks can't."

Turn 2: "That's right, studying does matter a lot. However, I still think a little housework can help us relax and manage our time better."

Turn 3: "I'd tell them that housework doesn't take much time at all. So, I still think it's worth doing a little every day."
🧠 Lên ý trước khi nói (không cần viết ra, chỉ cần nghĩ nhanh trong đầu theo đúng 4 bước đã học)
  1. Nêu ý kiến — should/shouldn't, dùng "I think.../In my opinion..."
  2. Lý do 1 — bắt đầu bằng "First,..."
  3. Lý do 2 — bắt đầu bằng "Also,..." hoặc "Second,..."
  4. Kết ý / phản hồi lại — dùng "However,..." hoặc "That's why..." để chốt ý hoặc phản hồi phản biện
(a) Athena Multi-turn
Athena — turn 1

"Some people say housework takes time away from studying. What's your opinion?"

Athena — turn 2, rebuttal

"But what if a student already has a lot of exams coming up?"

Athena — turn 3, rebuttal

"Interesting — why do you think that?"

Try to combine your opinion with 2 reasons using a connector (First,... Second,...), and combine agreeing/disagreeing with your reason in one fluent sentence.

"In my opinion, children should still do a little housework, even during exam time. First, it gives them practical experience that textbooks can't teach. Second, it helps them understand how much effort it takes to run a home. I agree that studying matters a lot, but I believe a short break for chores can actually help us relax and focus better."

Gợi ý 1 — Chủ đề khác: dọn vệ sinh lớp học

Do you think students should clean their own classroom, or should the school hire a cleaner? Give your opinion and explain why, using at least 2 reasons.

Theo bạn, học sinh có nên tự dọn vệ sinh lớp học, hay nhà trường nên thuê người dọn dẹp? Nêu ý kiến và giải thích bằng ít nhất 2 lý do.

🧩 Structure gợi ý:
1. Ý kiến (should/shouldn't)  →  2. Lý do 1 (First,...)  →  3. Lý do 2 (Also,...)  →  4. Kết (So,.../That's why...)
💬 Từ nối: First, Also, So, That's why
  • I stated my opinion clearly (should/shouldn't).
  • I gave 2 reasons, linked with a connector.
  • I used at least one expression from the Functional Language section.
Gợi ý 2 — Đóng vai

You are Minh. A friend says: "Housework is a waste of time — parents should just do it themselves." Respond and explain why you disagree.

🧠 Gợi ý ít hơn: chỉ cần nhớ 2 bước — Không đồng ý (Disagreeing) → Lý do.

  • I disagreed clearly.
  • I gave at least one reason.
  • I used a Disagreeing expression.
Gợi ý 3 — Phản hồi quote (không có gợi ý nào)
⚠️ Không có model answer hay gợi ý cấu trúc nào ở phần này — bạn tự nói hoàn toàn theo cách riêng của mình.

Quote: "Children who help at home grow up to be more responsible adults." Do you agree or disagree? Give one real example to support your answer.

  • I stated my opinion at the start.
  • I gave a real example.
  • My answer has a clear conclusion.

Self-evaluation

  • I can state an opinion using at least one expression from the Functional Language section.
  • I can give a clear opinion on whether children should do housework.
  • I can support my opinion with at least one specific reason.
  • I can agree or disagree with someone naturally.
