Card 1 · Benefit #1

Practical Skills

(3) First, it teaches me practical skills for real life. Last week, I cooked dinner for my family by myself, and I also cleaned my room without anyone asking. My mum said she felt happy because I could now take care of myself whenever I stayed home alone. Nobody teaches you these things in class — you only learn them by doing them again and again at home.

Card 2 · Benefit #2

Responsibility

(4) Second, chores help me build responsibility. Every Monday, I take out the rubbish without my parents telling me to. When a task belongs to me, there is no one else to do it instead, so I have to remember it myself. This makes me more careful and reliable, not only at home but in other parts of my life too.

Family Bond
